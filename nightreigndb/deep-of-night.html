Deep of Night

Exclusive mechanics, scaling, rewards, and progression details for Deep of Night expeditions.

Deep of Night Overview

A harder expedition mode with stronger enemies, exclusive rewards, and a ranking system built around Depth progression.

High Difficulty Mode
⚔️

Harder Expeditions

Enemies have more health, deal more damage, and may appear as stronger variants.

🏆

Rating System

Your Depth rating increases as you complete expeditions. Higher ratings result in greater difficulty.

👁️

Random Nightlord

The Nightlord is randomly selected each run. You can see which one it is at the start of the expedition on the character selection screen.

🌍

No Shifting Earth

You cannot choose the Shifting Earth in Deep of Night. It is generated randomly for each expedition.

🗡️

Deep Weapons

Special weapons can appear with exclusive effects, usually balanced by drawbacks.

🔮

Depths Relics

Earn relics exclusive to Deep of Night, often combining powerful bonuses with trade-offs.

🧩

Forsaken Hollows DLC

If you own the DLC, new Night Lords can appear as a possible Deep of Night target.

🤝

DLC Matchmaking

DLC bosses can only appear when matched with players who also own The Forsaken Hollows.

Rating System

Your Depth rating changes based on how well you do in each expedition. The higher your Depth, the harder future runs become.

Losses are based on how far you reached. Winning always grants points.

🛡️ Rank Protection

Starting at Depth 3, Rank Protection can prevent a loss from dropping you to a lower Depth.

Depth 3 1 protection per entry
Depth 4–5 2 protections per entry

If a loss would drop you below the current Depth threshold, the penalty is reduced so you stay at the minimum points for that Depth.

If you drop a rank and climb back again, your protections reset.
